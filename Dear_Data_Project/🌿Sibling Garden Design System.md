🌿Sibling Garden Design System

Event Description: Printed directly along the stem of the flower.



Type of Interaction: Determines the shape of the flower blossom.



Who Initiated: Represented by the number of leaves on the stem:



1 leaf = Initiated by You (Me)



2 leaves = Initiated by your Sister



Duration: Controls how tall the stem is (longer duration = taller stem).



Location: Determines the color of the leaves.











Time of Day: Determines the color of the stem.

Option A: The Sky Background / Ground Horizon

Morning / Afternoon: A small, bright Sun icon rises above or sits next to the flower.



Evening / Night: A small Crescent Moon or Stars float near the top of that flower's stem.



How it works: Every flower has a tiny celestial marker right next to its blossom indicating whether the event happened in daylight or at night.



Option B: The Flower Center (The "Eye" of the Blossom)

Instead of a plain round center, the center of the flower itself becomes the celestial body:



Sun Center: Golden yellow center with little radiating lines for daytime interactions.



Moon Center: Silver/pale-blue crescent shape or dark cratered circle for night/evening interactions.











Satisfaction / Positivity Scale: Determines which side the flower is offset to:



Right side = High positivity/satisfaction



Left side = Lower satisfaction/conflict

Bees: Swarming around any flower that scored a 10 out of 10 on satisfaction!









Moods (Me \& Her): Split flower center/shape—one half represents your mood and the other half represents her mood, with colors depending on the emotional state.



















Grid Layout: Arranged in neat rows and columns like a greenhouse or garden plot, complete with soil and a ground layer.



Stem Height: Determined by the Duration of the interaction (longer duration = taller stem).



Stem Color: Represented by the Time of Day (with cute Sun/Moon celestial markers).



Leaves on Stem: Represent Who Initiated (1 leaf = You / Me, 2 leaves = Your Sister).



Leaf Color: Mapped to the Location of the interaction.



Flower Offset (Left/Right): Mapped to the Satisfaction Scale (Right side = high satisfaction, Left side = lower satisfaction/conflict).



Bees: Swarming around any flower that scored a 10 out of 10 on satisfaction!



Flower Blossom Shape: Represented by the Type of Interaction.



Flower Center (Split Mood): Split down the middle—one half represents your mood, the other half represents her mood, with colors matching your emotional states.
